# Changelog

## 1.0 — public beta (September 2026)

First public release.

- Studio, Rehearse and Live modes; Home screen; guided tour; multiple stories.
- Paste or drop tables; bars, columns, line, KPI, table, donut and funnel; drill-down.
- PDF and image slides with background knock-out; dark, light and clear glass.
- Hand gestures: point, two-finger hold, page-turn swipe, pinch to move, two-hand resize, fist for the overview.
- Reframe yourself left or right; companion text card or second chart on the free side.
- One-file shared presentations that open to ▶ Play; recording; full workspace backup and restore.
- Engineering hardening for data safety, imports and chart accuracy — see `docs/hardening/`.
