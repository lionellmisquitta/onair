    2. Drop the PDF here.

    Settings

    Camera

    Camera off
    Hand tracking not loaded

    Laptop cameras centre you. Left or Right zooms in a little and slides the picture so the other side of the frame is free for your chart. The chart moves to that side automatically.

    Hands

    Directions are as you see them on screen. Page-turn feels like a book or a phone: draw the page toward you to move on.

    For the gesture study: what your hand did, what OnAir saw, and when.

    Gestures need one internet download for the hand model on first load. Your presentation data stays on your machine.

    Look

    Light glass suits slides exported with dark text. Dark glass suits charts and dim rooms. Clear draws the chart straight onto the video with a soft shadow — the “weatherman” look; best on plain, uncluttered backgrounds.

    Record

    Records the picture (and your voice) to a video file. The Record button is also in the top bar and on the Rehearse rail; V works anywhere.

    On the call

    1. Go live so only the picture is showing.
    2. In Teams / Zoom / Meet choose Share screen → share this browser window.
    3. Turn your own camera tile off. You're already on screen, with the chart.
    Want it in your camera tile instead? (OBS)
    1. Install OBS Studio. Add Source → Window Capture → this window.
    2. Start Virtual Camera.
    3. Pick “OBS Virtual Camera” as your camera in the call.
